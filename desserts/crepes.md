# Crepes
## Ingredients
1. flower
2. sugar
3. vanilla sugar
4. yeast
5. eggs
6. milk
7. butter
8. A lot of love

## Steps
1. Mix flower, sugar, vanilla sugar and yeast in a big bowl
2. Cover the floor and sides of the bowl with this mix
3. Break the eggs and mix them
4. Start stirring, then add a third of the milk
5. Keep stirring and when it feels too hard, add more milk
6. Meanwhile melt some butter
7. Once the mix is properly liquid, add butter
8. Cook the crepes on the pan
9. Enjoy!
